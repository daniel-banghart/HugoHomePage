# Introduction

Welcome. My name is Daniel (Dan in some circles).  I am a engineer of the software persuasion. I have worn many hats in my education and career. However, I mostly work in the realm of sofware with an interaction with hardware.

## Career

## Mercedes-Benz U.S. International

I began my career with Mercedes-Benz.  At MB, I worked on the software that coordinated the testing and commissioning of the vehicles.  My day to day activities included updating the software for the test procedures.  Updating testing software to adapt and conform to various configurations based on builds for the number of specialties and options avaialable on MB vehicles.  Much of the job entailed understanding the network topology of the vehicle and ensuring the testing and commissioning routines were able to execute despite the build conditon of the vehicle during manufacturing.  Often, dynamic problem solving had to be employed to work with Quality Engineering and Process Engineering departments to ensure that testing could effectively assess the vehicle quality at different points along the manufacturing line so as to find faults early.  

## Hewlett-Packard (HP Inc.)

I transitioned from manufacturing to technology.  At HP, I have been the technical lead in charge of designing and implementing a unified troubleshooting platform based on the experience I gained in my previous position.
